Curtains Pilot 1 — the two ways the Qualtrics survey ends
==========================================================

1) Participant did NOT consent  (End of Survey element in the "declined" branch)

   Customize → Custom end of survey message → paste:

   You have chosen not to take part in this study. No data from this session
   has been saved. Please close this page and return the study on Prolific.
   Returning a study does not affect your approval rating.

   Do NOT redirect this one to Prolific's completion URL.


2) Everyone else  (End of Survey element after the debriefing)

   Customize → Redirect to a full URL → paste, with your completion code:

   https://app.prolific.com/submissions/complete?cc=YOUR_COMPLETION_CODE

   (The completion code is shown in Prolific's study setup, under "Completion".)
